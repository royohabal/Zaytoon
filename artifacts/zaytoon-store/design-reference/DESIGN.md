## Brand & Style

This design system embodies modern editorial luxury, bridging heritage craftsmanship with contemporary avant-garde tailoring. Anchored in an editorial minimalist aesthetic, it draws inspiration from high-fashion ateliers: uncompromised whitespace, monumental typography, deliberate pacing, and structural discipline. 

The aesthetic marries classical sophistication with archival Palestinian textile heritage. Subtle geometric motifs inspired by tatreez structures appear only as ultra-fine hairline dividers, framing elements, and refined metadata badges. The emotional response is intentional, poised, and reverent—creating a calm, gallery-like viewing experience where the apparel remains the singular focal point.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in Mediterranean earth, sun-bleached limestone, and hand-spun linen. It is disciplined, restrained, and avoids oversaturation:

- **Primary (`#4A5844` - Zaytoon Deep Olive):** The anchor of the brand identity. Used for primary CTAs, refined headline accents, structural branding elements, and active states.
- **Secondary (`#C26D53` - Warm Terracotta):** Used sparingly as an accent for editorial highlights, seasonal badges, notification indicators, and delicate hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#D8CAB8` - Sandstone Beige):** Provides warm architectural grounding. Used for structural dividers, secondary surface cards, and background tint blocks.
- **Neutral (`#1C1C1A` - Deep Charcoal Ebony):** The primary color for typography, borders, and iconography, offering sharp legibility without the harshness of pure black.
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF8F5` - Crisp Raw Ivory):** The foundational backdrop across all viewports, providing a warm, tactile editorial presence that mimics uncoated heavy stock paper.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial cadence balancing classical romanticism with utilitarian precision:

- **Headings (Playfair Display):** Set with generous line-heights and restrained weights (regular 400 and medium 500). High contrast serifs deliver dramatic impact on editorial lookbooks, collection announcements, and hero layouts. Italicized cuts are reserved for editorial subtitles, cultural notes, and artisan origin quotes.
- **Body & Captions (Work Sans):** Rendered in light (300) and regular (400) weights to provide airy, modernist contrast against the serif titles.
- **Microcopy & Labels:** Always set in Work Sans uppercase with wide letter spacing (`0.12em` to `0.15em`) for product categories, collection tags, pricing, and sizing indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a high-fashion editorial grid: asymmetrical multi-column layouts, intentional negative space, and rigid alignments reminiscent of printed art monographs.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column layout on desktop with generous 40px gutters (`2.5rem`) and 80px side margins (`5rem`). On tablet, transitions to an 8-column grid with 24px gutters. On mobile, shifts to a 4-column structure with 20px margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Lookbook narratives utilize extreme vertical pacing (`space-2xl` and above) between collection chapters, forcing the eye to absorb silhouette, texture, and drapery before reaching accompanying metadata.
- **Reflow & Alignment:** Text blocks align flush left or center according to collection context, maintaining absolute vertical axis alignment with grid boundary lines. Full-bleed editorial spreads alternate with inset framed imagery.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids dropped drop-shadows and artificial blurs, opting instead for planar, architectural depth achieved through line, contrast, and surface layering:

- **Low-Contrast Outlines & Razor Borders:** Depth and containment are achieved through uniform 1px solid hairline borders (`#1C1C1A` at 15% to 100% opacity, or `#D8CAB8` for soft segmenting).
- **Surface Layering:** Elements elevate through chromatic steps: `#FAF8F5` (Base Ivory), layered with `#F3EFEA` (Sandstone Tint for utility drawers/modals), offset by deep primary contrasts (`#4A5844`).
- **Overlapping Planes:** In lookbook displays, depth is articulated through editorial overlapping—offset product imagery overlapping vertical hairline grids and caption containers without casting shadows.

## Shapes

The design system adheres to a strict `0` roundedness rule. Every element—buttons, form fields, image containers, badges, dialogs, and drawer menus—features razor-sharp 90-degree right angles (`0px` border radius). 

This absolute geometric purity references modern architectural design, printed broadsheets, and the crisp, linear nature of loom warp and weft fibers. Sharp geometric edges ensure visual cohesion alongside the hairline borders and high-contrast serifs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#4A5844` with crisp `#FAF8F5` typography, 0px border radius, 14px padding vertical by 32px horizontal. Text is set in `label-md` uppercase. Hover state shifts to `#1C1C1A` smoothly via a 200ms transition.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent with a 1px solid `#1C1C1A` border. Hover fills with `#4A5844` and flips text color to `#FAF8F5`.
- **Text Action:** Borderless, uppercase `label-md`, accompanied by a 1px underline positioned 4px below baseline that animates width from left to right on hover.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Product Card:** Borderless imagery flush with the grid, using 4:5 or 2:3 vertical aspect ratios. Title set in `headline-sm` or `body-md`, price in `label-md`. Quick-add and size selector reveal on hover via an architectural bottom slide-over in solid `#FAF8F5` with a 1px top border.
- **Lookbook Card:** Editorial image spanning multiple columns with an offset caption card framed in a 1px hairline border (`#D8CAB8`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Minimalist bottom-border-only style (1px solid `#1C1C1A` at 30% opacity) or full hairline box. Zero border-radius. On focus, the bottom border darkens to 1px solid `#4A5844`. Placeholder set in `body-sm` with light opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square geometries (0px radius). Checked state fills with `#4A5844` with a crisp ivory geometric check or inner square mark.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges (e.g., "Handwoven", "Pure Linen"):** Set in `label-sm` uppercase, enclosed in a 1px hairline border (`#4A5844` or `#C26D53`), with 4px vertical by 8px horizontal padding. Zero corner rounding.

### Navigation & Header
- **Top Bar:** Fixed, ultra-slender header with 1px bottom border in `#1C1C1A` (10% opacity). Minimal typography, centered wordmark in Playfair Display, and hairline iconography (search, bag, archival index).

### Heritage Badges & Tatreez Accents
- **Craftsmanship Stamp:** Square border badge enclosing metadata detailing artisan origin, weave density, and botanical dye batch. Accented with subtle 1px cross-stitch quadrant marks at the four card corners.
